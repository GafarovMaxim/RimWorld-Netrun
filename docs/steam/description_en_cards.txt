[b]Hacking Expansion: Netrun[/b] is a cyberpunk layer for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3573344880]Ushanka's Hacking Expansion[/url]. Every hack leaves a trace. Corporations notice. Fixers call. Dormant AIs wake up in your servers. From a cyberpod your netrunner walks a living map of cyberspace.

[quote][i]"The net remembers your trace. Data is currency, but the netrunner pays in body and mind."[/i][/quote]

[b]Hacking Expansion: Netrun[/b] is a cyberpunk layer for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3573344880]Ushanka's Hacking Expansion[/url]. Every hack leaves a trace. Corporations notice. Fixers call. Dormant AIs wake up in your servers. Deep in a cyberpod your netrunner walks a living map of cyberspace, and the net always answers.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GafarovMaxim/RimWorld-Netrun/v0.6.1/docs/steam/img/en_heat.png[/img]

Every intrusion raises the colony's [b]Heat[/b]: five levels from [b]Shadow[/b] to the [b]Red Zone[/b]. The hotter the trace, the harsher the answer: neural noise, data theft, machine sabotage, power surges, ransomware and raids by the hidden [b]Black Ledger[/b] mercenaries. Lay low, scrub the trace through a fixer, or burn bright and pay for it.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GafarovMaxim/RimWorld-Netrun/v0.6.1/docs/steam/img/en_terminal.png[/img]

The comms console becomes a [b]network console[/b]: a neon terminal with its own navigation screen of sections.
[list]
[*][b]Core[/b]: the Heat ring, trends, alerts, resources
[*][b]Club[/b]: fixers, corporations, crews, the top-100
[*][b]Board[/b]: contracts
[*][b]Archive[/b]: the trace journal
[*][b]Server room[/b]: your AIs
[*][b]Studio[/b]: braindance
[*][b]Gateway[/b]: the cyberspace map and dives
[/list]
A big zoomable [b]cyberspace map[/b] with lenses (loot, danger, corporations, jobs, time), search, routes and a watchlist. The [b]Cyberpedia[/b] explains everything with live examples, and a Guide mode outlines every part of the screen. Five sound packs, scaling and accent colours.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GafarovMaxim/RimWorld-Netrun/v0.6.1/docs/steam/img/en_cyberspace.png[/img]

One map per game: about 45 hubs in districts that you chart as you go. Corporate towers, markets, ruins, the campus, the hidden darknet and the [b]Old Net[/b] behind a wall. Runners in cyberpods walk its links in game time, up to three at once. Outages, jams, storms and new tenants: the net lives day by day. A free [b]training dive[/b] walks a newcomer through the dive screen.
[list]
[*][b]Turn-based dives[/b]: code gates, hidden caches, files, memory units, forks of easy and hard paths
[*][b]Black ICE[/b] strikes back with Hacking Expansion's nausea, lockout and coma
[*][b]Programs[/b] (Sword, Shield, Decoy, Stealth, Killer) and five [b]daemons[/b]; HE's ICE breakers and exec data fit right in
[*][b]Node alerts and ICE patrols[/b]: fail too often and the node wakes up
[*][b]Protected corporate hubs[/b]: find the key in a neighbouring hub first, then take the richer loot
[*][b]Corporate cores[/b]: boss nodes with the best loot in the net
[/list]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GafarovMaxim/RimWorld-Netrun/v0.6.1/docs/steam/img/en_fixers.png[/img]

A rotating roster of underworld [b]fixers[/b]: brokers, cleaners, fences and imperial clients (Royalty). Each hides a trait: reliable, greedy, paranoid, a collector... or a [b]rat[/b] who will sell you out. Build reputation, pin the ones you trust and take [b]8 kinds of contracts[/b]: data deliveries, system taps, quiet runs, surveillance, clean braindance labs and more.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GafarovMaxim/RimWorld-Netrun/v0.6.1/docs/steam/img/en_corps.png[/img]

[list]
[*][b]3 to 4 corporations[/b] own the corporate districts. Each has [b]Alert[/b] and [b]Strength[/b]: they harden their nodes, send patrols, launch intrusions along the map, capture territory and can [b]collapse[/b].
[*][b]Rival runner crews[/b] race you, help you, hunt contract targets and can be hired.
[*][b]The top-100 of the net[/b]: fame for every dive. The best get paid more, and watched more closely.
[/list]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GafarovMaxim/RimWorld-Netrun/v0.6.1/docs/steam/img/en_ais.png[/img]

Salvage AI fragments from the net and wake them up. [b]13 personalities[/b], from Ghost, Data Hunter and Muse to Predator, Oracle, Trickster, Seed and Echo, each with its own service, hunger, trust and traits.
[list]
[*]Keep them on an [b]AI server[/b] with racks, in a cyberpod [b]coprocessor[/b] or in a colonist's head through an [b]AI socket[/b]
[*][b]75 AI events[/b] with choices
[*][b]Personal quests[/b] in the vanilla Quests tab; AI levels unlock stronger and second services
[*]A colonist with a cyberlink who dies may leave an [b]Echo[/b]...
[/list]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GafarovMaxim/RimWorld-Netrun/v0.6.1/docs/steam/img/en_braindance.png[/img]

[b]Braindance goggles[/b] capture your colonists' strongest moments. Edit raw recordings into chips at the console, from normal to masterwork, or forbidden [b]black chips[/b]. Edit a whole pile in one click, if you dare the glitch. Sell them, watch them, get hooked. Hunt down [b]8 unique recordings[/b] hidden across the net.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GafarovMaxim/RimWorld-Netrun/v0.6.1/docs/steam/img/en_defense.png[/img]

Wall-mounted [b]firewalls[/b], your own [b]ICE tiers[/b] up to black ICE, a [b]honeypot[/b] that traces attackers back, netrunners on watch duty, full network isolation, and intercepting an incoming intrusion with a dive.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GafarovMaxim/RimWorld-Netrun/v0.6.1/docs/steam/img/en_transition.png[/img]

A late-game ending for colonies that live in the net. It starts with a rumour in the news feed and a stranger in the Club who used to work in a corporate black lab. What they built there, nobody outside knows. Raid the labs, piece the prototypes together, and decide who is ready to leave the flesh behind.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GafarovMaxim/RimWorld-Netrun/v0.6.1/docs/steam/img/en_colonists.png[/img]

[list]
[*][b]Netsick[/b]: pod addiction, cravings and reckless emotional dives (sometimes legendary); the pod tells how long a stay is still safe
[*]Dive traits, [b]9 backstories[/b], dive stories and tales
[*][b]Royalty[/b]: imperial fixers, a trace-scrub permit
[*][b]Ideology[/b]: net memes, rituals, the Net Archon role
[*][b]Biotech[/b]: a gene, braindance learning for children
[*][b]Odyssey[/b]: orbital hubs while your gravship is in space
[*][b]4 scenarios[/b]: The Last Node, Corporate Runaways, Pirate Studio, Flying Node (Odyssey)
[*][b]26 achievements[/b] with Vanilla Achievements Expanded (optional)
[/list]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GafarovMaxim/RimWorld-Netrun/dev/docs/steam/img/en_frames.png[/img]

[list]
[*][b]7 deck frames[/b] for the terminal, each with its own casing, keys and lamps that react to your Heat: the NR-1 Cyberdeck, Iron Company, Street, Kurenai Systems, Nomad, NR-80 Retro and the Relic. New frames open with achievements.
[*][b]Colour schemes[/b]: pick the terminal's accent colour
[*][b]Sound packs[/b]: Clean, Minimal, Neon, Retro Deck and Deep, separate volume for clicks and for each ambient loop; the game music fades while the terminal is open
[*][b]Graphics[/b]: animations, scanlines, glitches, the casing's lamps and LCD can each be turned off
[*][b]Fine-tuning[/b]: dozens of settings for balance and difficulty: Heat gain and decay, the frequency of network attacks, AI and cyberspace events, cyberspace size and layout, and many small things besides
[/list]
